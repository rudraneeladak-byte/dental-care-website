About the doctor

Dr Arindam Ganguly

BDS (Calcutta)

Dr Ganguly runs a general dental practice at Singur Club Building, treating families across Singur and the surrounding villages of Hooghly. The approach is straightforward: examine carefully, explain what is happening in plain language, and recommend the least invasive treatment that will hold up over time.

From a routine scaling to a root canal, crown or full denture, treatment is planned around what a patient actually needs and can commit to, with time set aside for questions before anything begins. Children, anxious patients and elderly patients are given the extra patience the visit calls for.

  • BDS, University of Calcutta Bachelor of Dental Surgery
  • Family and general dentistry Preventive, restorative and paediatric care under one roof
  • Evening clinic hours Open six days a week so appointments fit around work and school

Treatments

Comprehensive dental care

Preventive, restorative, cosmetic and emergency dentistry, carried out at the clinic with a clear plan and cost discussed in advance.

General check-ups

Full examination, X-rays where needed, and a written plan for anything that requires attention.

Cleaning and scaling

Ultrasonic scaling and polishing to clear plaque and tartar, with advice on keeping it away.

Fillings and restorations

Tooth-coloured composite fillings that seal decay and restore the natural shape of the tooth.

Root canal treatment

Single or multi-sitting endodontic treatment to save an infected tooth and settle the pain.

Crowns and bridges

Metal, ceramic and zirconia crowns, and fixed bridges to replace a missing tooth.

Implants and dentures

Single-tooth implants, partial and complete dentures, fitted and adjusted until they sit right.

Braces and orthodontics

Assessment and treatment for crowded, spaced or misaligned teeth, for teenagers and adults.

Emergency dental care

Severe pain, swelling, a broken tooth or a knocked-out tooth seen on the same evening where possible.
